About

I'm reading for two degrees in parallel — a BSc (Hons) in Information Technology & Management at the University of Moratuwa and a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science at the Informatics Institute of Technology. The combination is deliberate: one side teaches me how systems are built, the other how businesses decide what to build.

That's pushed me towards business analysis and quality assurance. Across my project work I've been the person mapping entities and relationships before anyone opened an editor, writing the acceptance criteria, and testing the thing at the end. I'm comfortable moving between a database schema and a conversation with someone who has never seen one.

Outside the coursework I play hockey — for my university, and previously for the Sri Lanka Schools team. Years of it have made me useful in the unglamorous parts of a project: turning up, coordinating people, and staying steady when a deadline gets close.
